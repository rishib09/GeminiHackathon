End-to-End Execution Sequence

Step-by-step lifecycle from title entry to live financial outputs

  1. 1

    Recipe Creation & Title Parsing

    User or Notion creates title: Compare volatility models on an American equity option. RootPicker.parse_title() extracts (root="EquityOption", verb=Verb.COMPARE, axis="volatility_model").

  2. 2

    Deterministic Scope Computation

    scope_walker.compute_scope() walks the knowledge graph DiGraph: 5 core needs, 4 varying volatility models, allowed formulas (price, delta, chain_rmse, rank), and siblings.

  3. 3

    Snapshot Integrity & Contract Resolution

    snapshots.store.load_snapshot() verifies the SHA-256 chain hash for spy_2026-09-10_eod and extracts the ATM 30-day put contract ($560 strike, 29 DTE, r=4.85%).

  4. 4

    Span Binding & Ambiguity Disambiguation

    compiler.binder scans recipe prose. Explicit words like "the spot", "risk-free rate", "local vol" are bound deterministically. Ambiguities like "American put" trigger interactive choice (In-Process vs OREApp XML).

  5. 5

    Dual-Mode ORE Execution & Ranking

    engine.ore_models.compare_volatility_models() runs FD Black-Scholes across all 4 models, computes RMSE across 412 contracts, and ranks the models (Local Vol / Heston rank 1-2).

  6. 6

    Notion Rendering & 5-Layer Explainer

    notion_ui.blocks formats Notion colored rich-text spans, embeds the live comparison results table, and links to the 5-layer grounded explainer document.
